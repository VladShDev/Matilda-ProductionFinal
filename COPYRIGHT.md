## What this document is not

This is a statement of the Owner's claim and a pointer to the record that
supports it. It is not legal advice and it is not a registration. Three things
the Owner should know, plainly:

- Copyright arises when a work is created and fixed; it needs no registration
  to exist, but registration (for example with the U.S. Copyright Office or the
  Owner's national office) strengthens enforcement. A lawyer can do this.
- Copyright protects the expression — the code, the texts, the recordings — not
  an idea, an algorithm or an architecture as such. Protecting the method as a
  method is the work of a patent, which has deadlines counted from the first
  public disclosure and needs a patent attorney. Keeping the repository private
  keeps that option open.
- Parts of the code and text were drafted with AI tools under the Owner's
  direction. In some jurisdictions purely machine-generated text has thin or no
  copyright of its own; what carries the claim is the Owner's design, his
  sentences, his selection and arrangement, and his corrections, which the
  history records.
