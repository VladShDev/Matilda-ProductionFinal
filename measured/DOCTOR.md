*"a doctor watches; she does not draw blood"* (only what a doctor sees from
outside is read: her eyes, her neck, her hands, her mouth's sounds, her
moving --- never her chemistry); when an item fails on a level of hers not

hum, her mother, time); *"no helpers"*. Every item runs on a birth from
